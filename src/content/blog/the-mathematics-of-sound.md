Sound waves can be described with surprisingly simple mathematics, and VocaSync reads the equations aloud.

## A single tone

A pure tone is a sine wave. We can write it as $y = A \sin(2 \pi f t)$, where $A$ is the amplitude and $f$ is the frequency.

## Energy

The energy carried by a wave grows with the square of its amplitude. In other words, $E \propto A^2$.

## Adding waves

When two tones combine, their amplitudes add together:

$$y(t) = A_1 \sin(2 \pi f_1 t) + A_2 \sin(2 \pi f_2 t)$$
